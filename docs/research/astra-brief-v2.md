Goal: "One More Run" (working title, likely to change with the theme) is a browser game built in roughly 19 remaining build hours by one engineer directing AI coding agents, presented live at a company hackathon at midday Friday 2026-09-18 to an audience of software engineers, some product managers, and a few UX designers. The destination was redrawn this morning. It is now a Hollow Knight-adjacent side-view action game. Intrigue comes from exploring a small interconnected area; difficulty comes from one well-designed boss plus a handful of meaningful non-boss enemies (every fight matters, never a swarm to mow down); rewards come from loot found by exploring or dropped by the boss, and possibly an equipment vendor. Verbs: attack, dash or dodge, jump; parry and further verbs are the stretch, and the vision iterates toward a full kit even though v1 cannot hold it all. There is no score and no timer: death respawns the player at a checkpoint. One boss is built, but to a broader vision: a world with themes, areas, and bosses that tie together, so the next area and boss are already designed on paper even if never built. The demo also tells the build story: a deployed build per milestone, an intervention log of every human code edit, and one or two transcript moments where an agent made a design call. The pivot itself is part of that story: a complete fixed-camera arena-survivor skeleton was built, verified, and deployed this morning in about 35 minutes of agent time, then retired because a colleague shipped a survivor clone at last year's hackathon and the audience would read ours as old hat.
